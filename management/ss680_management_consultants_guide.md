## 1. The Standard Overview

### 1.1 What SS 680 Is

**SS 680:2021, "Specification for management consultants"**, is a Singapore Standard (SS) published under the Singapore Standardisation Programme. Its official designation and title, as listed in the Singapore Standards eShop (the official shop of Enterprise Singapore's standards programme) and the National Library Board (NLB) catalogue, is: ✅

> **SS 680 : 2021 (ICS 03.080.20) — Specification for management consultants**

*[singaporestandardseshop.sg — SS 680:2021 product pages; nlb.gov.sg — catalogue entry]*

Three identifiers matter:

| Identifier | Value | Status |
|---|---|---|
| **Designation** | SS 680:2021 | ✅ Official shop listing |
| **Title** | Specification for management consultants | ✅ Official shop + NLB |
| **ICS code** | 03.080.20 | ✅ Printed on the official title block ("SS 680 : 2021 (ICS 03.080.20)") |

**ICS 03.080.20** is the International Classification for Standards (ICS) code under which SS 680 is classified — the 03.080 series covers *services*; 03.080.20 is the same classification family used for management-consultancy services internationally (ISO 20700:2017, the international management-consultancy guideline, is likewise classified under 03.080.20). ✅ *[Official SS 680 title block; ISO classification practice]* — note that the human-readable subcategory label for 03.080.20 varies across classification tables; the code itself is the verified fact.

**What kind of document it is.** An SS is a *national standard* — a Singapore Standard developed, promoted, and reviewed under the Singapore Standardisation Programme overseen by the Singapore Standards Council (Section 2.3). Unlike a guideline, SS 680 is titled a **Specification**, and in practice it functions as the reference standard for *personnel certification*: the Singapore certification schemes (SCMC, RMC — Section 4) assess individual management consultants against it. ✅

### 1.2 The Standard's Purpose

The official overview text published by the Singapore Standards eShop states the standard's purpose and coverage: ✅

> **"This standard provides a frame of reference for management consultancy. It sets out the consultancy process, competency and ethical requirements, and scope for continuous professional development for management consultants."**

*[singaporestandardseshop.sg — SS 680:2021 overview; identical framing in the TR 43:2015 overview it replaced]*

So the standard's purpose is to be **the Singapore frame of reference for the profession**: not a textbook on how to consult, but the agreed national definition of what management consultancy is, how a consultancy engagement should be run, what a consultant must be competent in, how a consultant must behave ethically, and how a consultant must keep developing. It is the answer to the question "what does 'professional management consultant' mean in Singapore?" — a question that matters for SMEs buying consulting services with government grant support (Section 4.4).

**Why Singapore needed this frame of reference.** The problem SS 680 (and TR 43 before it) exists to solve is an information-asymmetry problem in the SME consulting market. SMEs buying consulting services often cannot judge the quality of a consultant before engaging them — the service is intangible, the outcome is uncertain, and the buyer is less sophisticated than the seller. The SPRING-era framing was explicit: TR 43-aligned certification programmes would "provide SMEs a common set of references when selecting suitable management consultants" ✅ *[SPRING media release, July 2016]*. A national standard turns an unobservable quality (professionalism) into a checkable one (certified against SS 680). This is the same logic that drives professional standards in other domains this series covers — standards and certifications exist to make quality *inspectable* (`vendor_management_guide.md` treats the procurement side of this dynamic).

**A caution on what the standard is not.** ❌ SS 680 is not a licence to practise — nothing in the public record makes it a statutory gate for consulting work in general; and ❌ it is not a firm-level quality-management-systems certification (the individual-consultant orientation is verified in both the TR 43 wording — "individual management consultants" ✅ — and the personnel-certification schemes built on it). Misreadings of both kinds circulate in consultant marketing copy; the evidence tags in this guide are designed to help you spot them.

### 1.3 The Standard's Scope

The official overview continues with an explicit scope statement: ✅

> **"This standard covers aspects such as consultancy process, competence, ethics and continuous professional development."**

*[singaporestandardseshop.sg — SS 680:2021 overview; nlb.gov.sg catalogue record]*

The four scoped aspects:

1. **Consultancy process** — the defined way a consultancy engagement is structured and delivered (Section 5).
2. **Competence** — the competency requirements expected of a management consultant (Section 6).
3. **Ethics** — the ethical requirements / code of conduct expected of a consultant (Section 7).
4. **Continuous professional development (CPD)** — the scope for ongoing learning and professional upkeep (Section 8).

These four aspects are the spine of the entire guide: the standard is *process + competency + ethics + CPD*.

### 1.4 The Standard's Applicability

Who is SS 680 for? The official overview text states: ✅

> **"Compliance with SS 680:2021 (ICS 03.080.20) is applicable to management consultants in Singapore to ensure their services meet national standards for quality of practices."**

*[singaporestandardseshop.sg — SS 680:2021 overview (minor typo cleaned); nlb.gov.sg catalogue record]*

Three readings of "applicable":

- **As a compliance reference** — management consultants in Singapore who wish to demonstrate that their practice meets national quality standards can be assessed against SS 680 (this is what certification schemes operationalise, Section 4).
- **As a market signal** — the standard gives buyers of consulting services (especially SMEs) a common reference for selecting consultants, a purpose explicitly articulated for its predecessor TR 43:2015 ("SPRING-recognised certification programmes will provide SMEs a common set of references when selecting suitable management consultants" ✅ *[SPRING Singapore media release, July 2016, National Archives of Singapore]*).
- **Not as a licence-to-practise law** — ⚠️ nothing in the public record indicates SS 680 is a statutory licensing requirement for consulting in general. Its mandatory *force* enters through connected programmes such as the Enterprise Development Grant, which requires certified consultants for funded consultancy projects (Section 4.4), not through direct regulation of the consulting market. The actual level of voluntary adoption across the Singapore consulting population is ⚠️ not publicly measured — treat any "X% of consultants are certified" figure as unverifiable folklore.

## 2. The Standard History

### 2.1 The Predecessor: TR 43:2015

SS 680 did not appear from nowhere. Its direct predecessor is **TR 43:2015, the Technical Reference (TR) for management consultants** — the official Singapore Standards eShop explicitly lists TR 43:2015 with the note "Replaced by SS 680 : 2021". ✅ *[singaporestandardseshop.sg — TR 43:2015 product page]*

A **Technical Reference** is a provisional standard instrument in the Singapore system — a document published more quickly than a full Singapore Standard, typically to meet an urgent industry need, and intended to be upgraded to a full SS after a trial period (the TR instrument is a standard feature of the Singapore Standardisation Programme; the TR 43 → SS 680 path is a textbook example of that lifecycle). ✅

What we know about TR 43:2015 from official public sources:

- **Provenance:** "This Technical Reference was prepared by the Working Group (WG) on Consultant Quality Management under the direction of the Management System Standards Committee (MSSC). The MSSC endorsed the Technical Reference on 12 November 2015." ✅ *[TR 43:2015 preview/foreword, singaporestandardseshop.sg]*
- **Scope (identical in shape to SS 680):** "Provides a frame of reference for management consultancy. Defines the consultancy process, specifies the competency and ethical requirements and outlines the scope for continuous professional development of Management Consultants." ✅ *[TR 43:2015 overview, singaporestandardseshop.sg]*
- **Content emphasis:** "TR 43:2015 sets out the baseline competency, professional standards, ethical conduct and practices of individual management consultants." ✅ *[SPRING Singapore media release, "SPRING Singapore appoints three certification bodies to run certification programmes for management consultants", July 2016, National Archives of Singapore]*
- **Announcement:** First announced in May 2016, per the same SPRING release. ✅

Note the phrase "**baseline competency, professional standards, ethical conduct and practices of individual management consultants**" — TR 43 was always about *individual consultants*, not consulting firms. SS 680 inherits that orientation.

### 2.2 The Upgrade: SS 680:2021

**SS 680:2021** is the TR 43:2015 upgrade to full Singapore Standard status, published in 2021. ✅ *[singaporestandardseshop.sg — "Replaces TR 43 : 2015 - Technical Reference for management consultants"]*

- The **2021 year** is verified via the designation itself (SS 680:**2021**) across the official shop and NLB. ⚠️ The exact publication month/date in 2021 is not stated in the public snippets I could access — treat the year as verified, the month as not.
- The **content lineage** is verified: the overview text of SS 680:2021 is a direct continuation of TR 43:2015's — "frame of reference for management consultancy" + "consultancy process, competency and ethical requirements, and scope for continuous professional development" appears in both. ✅
- The **significance** of the upgrade: a TR is provisional; an SS is the durable national standard. By upgrading TR 43 → SS 680, Singapore signalled that consultant quality was a *permanent* national-standardisation concern, not a pilot. The certification schemes that had been built on TR 43 (Section 4) migrated to SS 680:2021 as the assessment basis — TÜV SÜD, operator of the SCMC scheme, states: "SS 680:2021 will be used for assessment for all new applications with immediate effect." ✅ *[tuvsud.com — Singapore Certified Management Consultant scheme page]*

### 2.3 Standards Development: Who Governs SS 680

SS 680 lives in Singapore's national standardisation architecture: ✅

- **Enterprise Singapore** — the government agency (under the Ministry of Trade and Industry) that administers the national standardisation and accreditation programmes. "As the national standards body, Enterprise Singapore oversees the Singapore Standardisation Programme through an industry-led SSC." ✅ *[enterprisesg.gov.sg — Singapore Standards Council page]*
- **The Singapore Standards Council (SSC)** — the industry-led council that develops, promotes, and reviews Singapore Standards and Technical References. "The SSC collaborates with key stakeholders including industry, professional bodies and government agencies to advise on policies, strategies, initiatives and procedures to develop, promote and review national standards. The SSC represents Singapore in key international standards bodies." ✅ *[enterprisesg.gov.sg; iso.org — SSC member page: "The Singapore Standards Council facilitates the development, promotion and review of Standards and Technical References in Singapore... under the national standardisation programme administered by Enterprise Singapore"]*
- **Standards committees and working groups** — the hands-on drafting work happens in sector committees and working groups (for TR 43:2015 it was the Working Group on Consultant Quality Management under the Management System Standards Committee ✅ — the exact committee lineage for SS 680:2021 itself is ⚠️ not itemised in the public snippets I could access, but the TR 43 lineage shows the mechanism).
- **Historical note on the agency name:** TR 43:2015 was announced by **SPRING Singapore** ("SPRING Singapore appoints three certification bodies..." ✅ *[NAS media release, July 2016]*). SPRING Singapore merged with IE Singapore to form **Enterprise Singapore** in April 2018 — a well-documented public fact ✅ — which is why current pages carry the EnterpriseSG/SSC branding. This explains why older documents reference SPRING and newer ones reference Enterprise Singapore for the same programme family.

**Why governance matters for the reader:** because SS 680 is a Singapore Standard (not a private scheme's proprietary standard), it carries the authority of the national standardisation body — which in turn is why government grants (EDG) and government-recognised certification programmes (RMC) can lean on it as the common reference.

## 3. The Standard and ISO 20700

### 3.1 ISO 20700:2017 — The International Equivalent

**ISO 20700:2017, "Guidelines for management consultancy services"**, is the international standard for the management-consultancy profession: ✅

> "ISO 20700:2017 provides guidelines for the effective delivery of management consultancy services."

*[iso.org — ISO 20700:2017 standard page; edition 1, 2017; last reviewed and confirmed 2022, so it remains current]*

Verified facts about ISO 20700:

- **Published 1 June 2017**, after a three-year development project led by the International Council of Management Consulting Institutes (ICMCI) — the global body of national management-consulting institutes, of which Singapore's IMCS is a member ✅ *[cmc-global.org announcement; iso.org]*
- **Developed by ISO project committee ISO/PC 280, Management consultancy** ✅ *[iso.org; the standard's own foreword: "This document was prepared by Project Committee ISO/PC 280, Management consultancy"]*
- **A guideline, not a requirements standard**: it "offers practical guidelines to both clients and management consultants to ensure both parties are clear on what is required for an effective and efficient consultancy process, the objectives and their respective responsibilities" ✅ *[Wikipedia: ISO 20700]*. Because it is a *guideline*, it is not itself a certification standard — you cannot be "ISO 20700 certified" in the way you can be certified to a requirements standard.
- **European adoption**: CEN published EN ISO 20700:2018 and withdrew the older European standard EN 16114 in 2019; ISO 20700 correlates with EN 16114 ✅ *[Wikipedia: ISO 20700]*
- **Verified structure** (from the standard's own table of contents and body text, which is publicly available):
  - Clause 1–3: Scope, Normative references, Terms and definitions
  - **Clause 4: Principles** — including 4.4.3 *Code of ethical and professional conduct*, 4.4.6 *Communication*, 4.4.7 *Data protection and confidentiality*, 4.4.8 *Protection of intellectual property*, 4.4.11 *Risk and quality management* — and 4.1.2 *Typical consulting process* ✅ *[ISO 20700:2017(E) text]*
  - **Clause 5: Contracting** — 5.5.2 Context, 5.5.3 Services and deliverables, 5.5.4 Approach and work plan, 5.5.5 Roles and responsibilities, 5.5.6 Acceptance criteria, 5.5.7 Terms and conditions ✅
  - **Clause 6: Execution** — 6.5.2 Refining the agreed work plan, 6.5.3 Implementing the work plan, 6.5.4 Assignment management and monitoring, 6.5.5 Approvals and acceptance ✅
  - **Clause 7: Closure** — 7.5 Contents: legal and contractual matters, and more ✅

### 3.2 The Relationship: SS 680 vs ISO 20700

**The honest framing first:** the exact textual relationship between SS 680:2021 and ISO 20700:2017 — whether SS 680 was drafted to align clause-by-clause with ISO 20700, borrowed from it, or developed independently — is ⚠️ **not documented in any public source I could access** (the SS 680 text itself is paywalled, and neither the shop overview nor the scheme pages state a formal alignment). Treat any claim of a specific "SS 680 is aligned to ISO 20700 clause X" as unverified.

**What *is* verified about the relationship:**

- **Same subject, same classification.** Both are standards for management consultancy services, both classified under ICS 03.080.20, both cover the consultancy process. ✅
- **Same architecture of content.** ISO 20700 = principles + process (contracting → execution → closure). SS 680 = process + competency + ethics + CPD. The overlap is real: process and ethics appear in both; SS 680's distinctive additions are *competency requirements* and *CPD scope* — the individual-consultant dimensions that TR 43 introduced in Singapore ("baseline competency... of individual management consultants" ✅). ISO 20700, by contrast, addresses the *service* level (MCSP = management consultancy service provider, which can be a firm) rather than certifying individuals. ✅/⚠️ — the firm-vs-individual reading of ISO 20700's audience is supported by its text ("people or organizations"); the *contrast* with SS 680's individual orientation is supported by TR 43/SS 680's public wording.
- **Different instruments, different uses.** ISO 20700 is an international *guideline* (informative, not certifiable); SS 680 is a national *specification* used as the assessment basis for certification schemes (SCMC, RMC). In practice: a consultant in Singapore gets *certified to SS 680*; ISO 20700 is the international point of reference for how consultancy services should be managed. ✅
- **Institutional link via ICMCI.** ISO 20700 was developed under ICMCI leadership, and IMCS (Singapore) is an ICMCI/CMC-Global member — so the Singapore professional body sits inside the same international network that produced ISO 20700. ✅ This is the closest documented institutional connection between the two; the *technical* alignment remains ⚠️.
- **A market misreading to correct.** Some secondary sources (consultant marketing pages, grant-guide aggregators) claim SS 680 "certifies consultancy firms' quality management systems" or describe TR 43/SS 680 as firm-level QMS certification. That is ❌ **not supported** — both TR 43 and SS 680 are about *individual management consultants* ("baseline competency... of individual management consultants" ✅; SCMC and RMC are personnel certifications issued to individuals ✅). Read such sources with suspicion.

Read this list with the relationship caveat from 3.2 in mind: these are ISO 20700's *verified* principles. ## 4. The Certification Ecosystem

SS 680 is not a shelf document. Its practical force comes from the ecosystem of certification schemes, accreditation bodies, and grants built around it. This section maps that ecosystem: the two main certification schemes (SCMC, RMC), the accreditation framework (SAC) that keeps them honest, and the grant (EDG) that makes them commercially decisive.

### 4.1 SCMC — Singapore Certified Management Consultant (TÜV SÜD)

**SCMC (Singapore Certified Management Consultant)** is a certification scheme for individual management consultants operated by **TÜV SÜD PSB** (the Singapore arm of TÜV SÜD, the German testing/certification group; today operating as TÜV SÜD Pte Ltd). ✅

Verified facts from the scheme's official page:

- The scheme provides "SCMC certification for management consultants in Singapore, assessing competence and professional standards under SS 680:2021." ✅ *[tuvsud.com — Singapore Certified Management Consultant scheme page]*
- On the migration to the new standard: "SS 680:2021 will be used for assessment for all new applications with immediate effect." ✅ *[tuvsud.com]* — i.e., the SCMC scheme assesses candidates against SS 680:2021 (having previously assessed against TR 43:2015; the scheme's certificate history references "TR 43:2015 / SS 680:2021" per certified-consultant announcements ✅/⚠️ *[e.g. SCMC award announcements citing both standards]*).
- **Who certifies vs who accredits:** SCMC certificates are issued by TÜV SÜD (the certification body); TÜV SÜD's right to run personnel certification rests on its **accreditation** by the Singapore Accreditation Council (SAC) under **ISO/IEC 17024** (the international standard for personnel-certification bodies) — TÜV SÜD's Singapore accreditations list includes ISO/IEC 17024. ✅ *[tuvsud.com — Accreditations and Approvals in Singapore; sac-accreditation.gov.sg]* (Precise scope of the SAC accreditation specific to the SCMC scheme: ⚠️ the accreditation-register detail was not extractable, but the SAC-accredited status of the personnel-certification scheme is corroborated by multiple independent sources.)

### 4.2 RMC — Registered Management Consultant (IMCS)

**RMC (Registered Management Consultant)** is the second pillar — a certification programme run by the **Institute of Management Consultants Singapore (IMCS)**, the Singapore professional body for management consultants and a member of the global **ICMCI/CMC-Global** network. ✅

Verified facts from the official IMCS page:

> "The Registered Management Consultants (RMC) is an Enterprise Singapore-recognised certification programme for management consultants aligned to SS 680:2021 Specification for Management Consultants. The programme was developed by Certified Management Consultants from the Institute of Management Consultants (Singapore) with the objectives of recognising skills, knowledge and competency in the [consulting profession]..."

*[imcs.sg — RMC programme page]*

Three verified points stand out:

- **Enterprise Singapore-recognised**: the RMC carries explicit recognition from the national agency — the same agency that runs the standardisation programme and the EDG grant. ✅
- **Aligned to SS 680:2021**: the programme's assessment basis is the standard itself. ✅
- **Developed by Certified Management Consultants from IMCS**: the scheme was built by practitioners certified under the international CMC (Certified Management Consultant) credential, giving it a lineage to the global ICMCI/CMC system. ✅

The accreditation chain for RMC is confirmed by an authoritative non-IMCS source — a Straits Times forum letter (January 2024) on certification for consultants serving grant-supported SMEs: "IMCS is a Singapore Accreditation Council-accredited certification body for the Registered Management Consultant (RMC), a certification programme aligned to the SS680:2021 standard which sets out..." ✅ *[straitstimes.com — Forum, 17 Jan 2024]*. So: **IMCS = certification body, SAC = accreditor, SS 680 = the standard, EnterpriseSG = recognition + the grant link.**

### 4.3 The Certification Bodies and the SAC Framework

**SAC — the Singapore Accreditation Council** — is Singapore's national accreditation body. Verified facts: ✅

- "SAC is the national accreditation body. Managed by Enterprise Singapore, SAC maintains the integrity and impartiality of conformity assessment practices." ✅ *[sac-accreditation.gov.sg; SAC LinkedIn/EnterpriseSG pages]*
- Accreditation provides "third party assurance that testing, inspection and certification are conducted professionally and meet globally-recognised standards." ✅ *[sac-accreditation.gov.sg]*
- For personnel certification, the globally recognised standard is **ISO/IEC 17024** — and both TÜV SÜD (SCMC) and IMCS (RMC) sit in the SAC-accredited certification-body framework under it. ✅/⚠️

The distinction to keep straight — **certification vs accreditation** (see Glossary):

- A **certification body** (TÜV SÜD, IMCS) *certifies individuals* — issues SCMC/RMC certificates after assessing a consultant against SS 680.
- An **accreditation body** (SAC) *accredits the certification bodies* — verifies that their assessment processes are competent and impartial, per ISO/IEC 17024 and related criteria.
- One more historical anchor: SPRING Singapore appointed **three certification bodies** in 2016 to run the original TR 43-aligned programmes ✅ *[NAS media release, July 2016]* — the exact third body in that 2016 list is ⚠️ not verifiable from the snippets I could parse (the archive PDF requires a browser session), but TÜV SÜD PSB and IMCS are the two that are verifiably operating SCMC and RMC today.

### 4.4 EDG — The Enterprise Development Grant and the Certified-Consultant Requirement

**EDG (Enterprise Development Grant)** is an Enterprise Singapore grant that "supports projects that help you upgrade, innovate, grow and transform your business," funding "qualifying project costs namely third-party consultancy fees, software and equipment, and internal manpower cost." ✅ *[enterprisesg.gov.sg — Enterprise Development Grant page]*

The link to SS 680: **for consultancy-related project costs, SMEs applying for EDG must engage certified management consultants.** ✅/⚠️ Verification status:

- ✅ The *existence* of a certified-consultant requirement is well documented: the Straits Times forum letter (Jan 2024) is titled around "Certification needed for management consultants working with SMEs that apply for grant" and discusses the RMC/SS 680 requirement for EDG-supported consultancy work. ✅
- ✅ The *direction* is corroborated by a wide set of independent industry sources ("SMEs applying for EDG support for consultancy-related costs must engage certified management consultants"; "EDG management consultancy projects require a consultant with SAC-accredited TR 43 or SS 680 certification"). ✅/⚠️ — secondary but consistent.
- ⚠️ The *precise operational detail* — exactly which certifications (RMC, SCMC, and/or others such as the Practising Management Consultant, PMC) EnterpriseSG accepts, for which EDG project categories, and at which dates — lives in EDG guidelines that have evolved over time (TR 43-era certifications were grandfathered into the SS 680 era). I could not extract the authoritative grant-guideline page in full, so treat the *operational mechanics* as scheme-guideline detail, and the *principle* (certified consultant for EDG-funded consultancy) as verified.

**Why this matters:** the EDG link is the economic engine of the whole ecosystem. A consultant's SS 680-based certification is not just a badge — it determines whether an SME can claim grant support for that consultant's fees. This is why SS 680 certification is commercially meaningful in Singapore's SME-consulting market in a way that a generic professional credential is not.

### 4.6 How to Verify a Consultant's Certification

For anyone buying consulting services (or for a consultant choosing which scheme to pursue), the practical question is: *how do I know a "certified consultant" claim is real?* The verified checks, based on the ecosystem structure:

1. **Ask which scheme and which standard.** A legitimate claim names the scheme (SCMC or RMC) and the assessment basis (SS 680:2021 — or TR 43:2015 for certifications issued before the migration). Vague claims ("certified management consultant" with no scheme) prove nothing. ✅/⚠️
2. **Ask who issued it.** SCMC certificates come from TÜV SÜD PSB (TÜV SÜD Pte Ltd); RMC certificates from IMCS. A certificate from a body that is not an SAC-accredited certification operator for the scheme is a red flag. ✅ *[tuvsud.com; imcs.sg]*
3. **Ask about currency.** Certifications under ISO/IEC 17024-style schemes are maintained via ongoing CPD and periodic renewal — a "certified" claim should come with a current certificate and active CPD status. ⚠️ (renewal mechanics are scheme-specific)
4. **Check the standard, not the marketing.** If a consultant claims SS 680 "certifies their firm" or quotes "SS 680 clause" numbers, treat the claim as ❌/⚠️ — SS 680 certifies individuals, and its internal clauses are not publicly quotable.
5. **For EDG purposes, confirm with the grant rules.** Which certifications EnterpriseSG currently accepts for which EDG project categories is grant-guideline detail (⚠️ evolving); the scheme operators and EnterpriseSG's current EDG materials are the authoritative checkpoints.

## 5. The Consultancy Process

### 5.1 What the Standard Says — and What It Does Not (Publicly) Say

The official scope text says SS 680 "defines the consultancy process." ✅ *[singaporestandardseshop.sg — SS 680:2021 overview]* Beyond that single sentence, the **internal clause structure of the standard's process section is paywalled** — the public record does not reveal SS 680's own phase names, clause numbers, or process requirements. ⚠️ **This is the single most important flag in this guide: no publicly accessible source itemises SS 680's internal process clauses; anything you read elsewhere claiming to quote "SS 680 clause 5.3" or similar should be treated as unverifiable.**

So how do we responsibly present "the consultancy process" of SS 680? Three verified anchors:

1. **SS 680's scope includes the consultancy process** — verified, quoted above. ✅
2. **The TR 43 lineage shows the process was always a core pillar** — TR 43:2015 "defines the consultancy process" in the same words. ✅
3. **The international frame for the same content is fully public** — ISO 20700:2017 (the sibling standard, Section 3) documents the consultancy process in detail, and its text is publicly available. The task's framing — **propose → deliver → close** — corresponds to ISO 20700's documented process: **contracting → execution → closure**. ✅

The honest, useful presentation therefore uses ISO 20700's *verified* process as the documented international frame for what "the consultancy process" means in a management-consultancy standard, with the explicit caveat that SS 680's own internal expression of it is ⚠️ unverifiable-without-purchase.

### 5.2 The Process: Propose → Deliver → Close (per ISO 20700)

From the ISO 20700:2017 text (Clause 4.1.2, "Typical consulting process"):

> "The consulting process includes three typical activities: contracting, execution and closure..."
>
> — **Contracting**: "The client and MCSP reach a clear agreement on the scope and approach of the service to be provided and commit to working together." (Clause 5)
> — **Execution**: "Once agreement has been reached, the next part of the assignment is execution. The MCSP provides the service in accordance with the agreed approach and plan. The MCSP and the client need to collaborate with confidence, fairness and mutual respect to achieve a mutually positive outcome." (Clause 6)
> — **Closure**: "The final stage of the assignment is closure. After completion of the assignment, including the delivery of all deliverables and assessment of the results, the assignment is 'closed' by resolving any unfinished items and meeting all contractual and legal obligations. It is critical that the MCSP gains acceptance from the client that the assignment is complete." (Clause 7)

*[ISO 20700:2017(E), 4.1.2, 5, 6, 7 — quoted from the standard text]* ✅

Note the professional-responsibility allocation, which ISO 20700 states explicitly: "The MCSP is responsible for its resources and work, however the ultimate accountability for decisions, outcomes, deliverables and the impacts on stakeholders lies with the client." ✅ *[ISO 20700:2017(E), 4.1.3]*

This maps cleanly to the propose → deliver → close framing, and to how the rest of this series describes engagements: the scoping/proposal stage (`management_consulting_skills_guide.md` covers proposal storytelling; `mckinsey_approach_guide.md` covers the structured delivery methodology; `forward_deployed_engineering_guide.md` covers an embedded-delivery variant of the same arc).

### 5.3 Process Table

| Phase | Activities (documented international frame) | Deliverables / Outcomes |
|---|---|---|
| **Propose / Contracting** (Clause 5) | Understand the client's context; agree scope, services and deliverables; define approach and work plan; fix roles and responsibilities; agree acceptance criteria; settle terms and conditions ✅ *[ISO 20700 5.5.2–5.5.7]* | Signed agreement covering context, deliverables, work plan, roles, acceptance criteria, terms ✅ |
| **Deliver / Execution** (Clause 6) | Refine the agreed work plan; implement it; manage and monitor the assignment; obtain approvals and acceptance at milestones ✅ *[ISO 20700 6.5.2–6.5.5]* | Delivered work products; progress reporting; approved milestones; client acceptance ✅ |
| **Close / Closure** (Clause 7) | Deliver all deliverables; assess results; resolve unfinished items; meet contractual and legal obligations; gain client acceptance that the assignment is complete ✅ *[ISO 20700 7.1–7.5, 4.1.2]* | Formal closure; acceptance sign-off; outstanding items resolved; legal/contractual matters settled ✅ |
| **SS 680's own expression of the above** | ⚠️ Paywalled — not publicly documented | ⚠️ Unverifiable without purchasing SS 680:2021 |

CPD and renewal** | To maintain the certificate, she completes continuous professional development activities (training, conferences, learning — Section 8) on an ongoing cycle. ⚠️ Scheme-specific CPD rules. | CPD is a named pillar of SS 680 ✅ *[official overview]*; certification maintenance via CPD is standard scheme practice ✅/⚠️ |

### 9.3 The EDG Context

With her certification in hand, Mei Ling's SME clients can now engage her for EDG-supported consultancy projects:

- The clients apply to **Enterprise Singapore's EDG** for projects that "help you upgrade, innovate, grow and transform your business," with EDG funding "qualifying project costs namely third-party consultancy fees, software and equipment, and internal manpower cost." ✅ *[enterprisesg.gov.sg]*
- For the *consultancy-fee* component, the certified-consultant requirement applies: SMEs need certified management consultants for grant-supported consultancy work. ✅/⚠️ *[Straits Times forum, Jan 2024; consistent industry corroboration]*
- Mei Ling's SS 680-based certification is therefore not decorative — it is the difference between her fees being claimable under EDG and not. That is the practical force of the standard in the SME market.

### 9.4 The Lessons

1. **Certification is the standard's operating system.** SS 680 defines the profession; the schemes (SCMC/RMC) certify against it; SAC accredits the certifiers; EDG creates the demand. A consultant who only reads the standard without understanding this loop misses how SS 680 actually works. ✅
2. **The standard is a quality signal, not a marketing badge.** For SMEs, an SS 680-aligned certification is a shortcut to trust — a third-party, accredited assessment that the consultant meets a national definition of process, competence, ethics, and CPD. For consultants, it converts practice quality into a portable, auditable credential. ✅/⚠️ (the *effect* on selection outcomes is documented only anecdotally — treat any quantified claim as unverified)
3. **Buyers should verify, not assume.** "Certified" should mean: certified by an SAC-accredited certification body (TÜV SÜD for SCMC, IMCS for RMC) against SS 680:2021, with the certificate current (CPD maintained). ❌ Beware marketing that claims SS 680 "certifies firms" — it certifies individual consultants. ✅/❌
4. **The paywall is real.** Anyone — buyer, consultant, or researcher — who needs SS 680's internal requirements (process clauses, competency matrix, ethics code, CPD rules) must purchase the standard from the Singapore Standards eShop. Public summaries (including this guide) are built from the official overview text plus the documented ecosystem, and say so. ⚠️
5. **For the architect-reader (this series' lens):** the SS 680 ecosystem is a working example of *standards-driven professionalisation* — a national standard, a certification market, an accreditation referee, and a grant engine aligning incentives. The same pattern (standard → certifier → accreditor → incentive) is visible in IT professionalism; see `analytics_management_guide.md` and `vendor_management_guide.md` for adjacent procurement/standards dynamics.

### 9.5 Common Pitfalls on the Journey

The certification journey has well-trodden failure modes — flagged here so the example doesn't mislead:

1. **Confusing the two schemes.** SCMC (TÜV SÜD PSB) and RMC (IMCS) are *different* programmes with different operators, histories, and recognition bases — though both assess against SS 680:2021. Choosing one is a commercial decision (which recognition your EDG-linked clients value), not a trivia question. ✅/⚠️
2. **Chasing "SS 680 certification" as a single credential.** There is no unified "SS 680 certificate" issued by EnterpriseSG or the Standards Council — certification is issued by the *certification bodies* (TÜV SÜD, IMCS) against the standard. Anyone offering "the official SS 680 certificate" directly is misrepresenting the architecture. ✅/⚠️
3. **Neglecting CPD maintenance.** Certification is a state, not a one-time event; the standard's CPD pillar exists precisely so that certification decays if development stops. Scheme-level CPD rules must be tracked from day one. ⚠️
4. **Marketing over-claims.** Claiming firm-level certification, quoting unverifiable "SS 680 clause" content, or implying statutory licence status are all ❌ misreadings that a knowledgeable buyer (Section 4.6) will catch.
5. **Ignoring the EDG rule evolution.** The certified-consultant requirement and its accepted-certification list have evolved from the TR 43 era into the SS 680 era; a consultant's plans should be checked against *current* EnterpriseSG grant materials at the time of engagement. ⚠️

## 10. Summary: SS 680 in One Page

**What it is.** SS 680:2021, "Specification for management consultants" (ICS 03.080.20), is the Singapore Standard that provides the national frame of reference for management consultancy. Its scope is four pillars: **consultancy process, competency, ethical requirements, and continuous professional development**. Compliance is applicable to management consultants in Singapore so their services meet national standards for quality of practice. ✅ *[official overview text]*

**Where it came from.** SS 680:2021 is the upgrade of **TR 43:2015**, the Technical Reference for management consultants (endorsed November 2015, announced May 2016, with SPRING Singapore appointing three certification bodies in July 2016). Governance sits with the **Singapore Standards Council** under Enterprise Singapore. ✅

**Its international sibling.** **ISO 20700:2017** (Guidelines for management consultancy services, developed under ICMCI/ISO/PC 280, published June 2017) is the international guideline for the profession — same subject, same ICS family, same process orientation (contracting → execution → closure). SS 680 is the national specification used for certification; the formal textual alignment between the two is ⚠️ not publicly documented. ✅ for everything else.

**The ecosystem.** Two mainstream certification schemes assess individual consultants against SS 680:2021 — **SCMC** (Singapore Certified Management Consultant, TÜV SÜD PSB) and **RMC** (Registered Management Consultant, IMCS, Enterprise Singapore-recognised). Both operate under **SAC** (Singapore Accreditation Council) accreditation (personnel certification, ISO/IEC 17024 framework). The commercial engine is **EDG** (Enterprise Development Grant): SMEs applying for EDG support for consultancy projects must engage certified consultants. ✅/⚠️

**What it demands.** The standard covers the consultancy process (internationally documented as propose/contract → deliver/execute → close; SS 680's internal clauses are paywalled), competency (assessed by the certification bodies), ethics (integrity, objectivity, confidentiality, competence, professional behaviour — as general consulting ethics; SS 680's specific code is paywalled), and CPD (ongoing development; scheme-level rules). ⚠️ internal details flagged throughout.

**The final word.** SS 680 is **the Singapore standard for consulting quality** — the rare standard that connects a national definition of professionalism (process + competency + ethics + CPD) to a working certification market (SCMC/RMC), an accreditation referee (SAC), and a grant that makes certification commercially decisive (EDG). For consultants, it is the route to a credible quality signal; for buyers, it is the reference for choosing who to trust; for anyone reading the standard's claims in the market, the rule is: *verify against the official text and the scheme operators — the standard itself is paywalled, and the ecosystem around it is the part that is publicly verifiable.*
