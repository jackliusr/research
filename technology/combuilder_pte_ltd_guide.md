## 1. Company Overview

### 1.1 The Incorporation — 24 November 1999 (Verified)

Combuilder Pte Ltd was incorporated in Singapore on **24 November 1999** (a Wednesday, per sgpbusiness.com) — verified against six independent ACRA-derived registry aggregators (companies.sg, opengovsg.com, sgpbusiness.com, sgbizverify.com, recordowl.com, sg.ltddir.com), which agree exactly on the date. The UEN **199907364N** encodes the registration year directly in its leading digits (see §1.2). As of the August 2026 research pass, companies.sg computes the company as ~26.8 years old — the oldest subject in this company-profile series by a wide margin (Cedars Digital: 2023; Bidot Tech: 2019; the Bond entities: 2024-vintage).

What this means in context: Combuilder is a **1999-vintage Singapore company** — it incorporated in the pre-dot-com-bust era, before the "Gen 3 / AI-native" startup wave described in [singapore_saas_companies_guide.md](singapore_saas_companies_guide.md) §1.4, and it belongs to the *services* tier of the Singapore IT economy: systems integration and software development sold as projects and, later, as manpower. It is **not** a shell, a startup, or a struck-off defunct entity — it has a continuous live registry record, current filing obligations, a working website, and active hiring (§5). For the series context, the important date is the other way around: when the author worked there as Team Leader (~2008–2010, §6), the company was already **9–11 years old** — an established small systems-integration firm, not a young startup.

### 1.2 The UEN — 199907364N (Verified)

The Unique Entity Number **199907364N** is verified via all six primary aggregators plus sgp.databasesets.com (seven sources). The UEN structure is the **legacy ROC-number format that became the UEN**: the leading **1999** encodes the incorporation year, **07364** is the sequence within that year's issue block, and the trailing **N** is the checksum letter. This is the standard pattern for companies incorporated before the ACRA UEN scheme (introduced 2009): their old Registry of Companies numbers were converted into UENs.

**Flag:** one aggregator (sgp.databasesets.com) prints a "UEN Issue Date" of **11 Sep 2008**, while opengovsg.com prints **24 Nov 1999** — the former is most plausibly a legacy-number-conversion timestamp from the UEN rollout rather than the statutory date, but the guide flags the discrepancy rather than adjudicating it. The statutory incorporation date (24 Nov 1999) is what the six primary aggregators agree on.

For a name as generic as "Combuilder" — a name that collides with at least four other "Combuilder"-prefixed Singapore entities (§1.8) — **the UEN, not the name, is the entity's safe identifier**, exactly the discipline the Bond guides (§1.2 in each) and the Cedars guide establish for their subjects, and the discipline a bank's vendor-onboarding team would apply.

### 1.3 The Entity Type — Exempt Private Company Limited by Shares (Verified)

The registry classifies Combuilder as a **Local Company** with company type description **"Exempt Private Company Limited by Shares"** — verified via companies.sg, opengovsg.com, sgpbusiness.com, sgbizverify.com and recordowl.com, which agree. Key implications, all verified registry/statutory facts:

- An **exempt private company (EPC)** is a private company limited by shares with **no more than 20 members** and **no corporate member** (with narrow exceptions) under the Companies Act (Cap. 50). Combuilder's recordowl record lists **1,000,000 ordinary shares** and a shareholder count that changed **4 → 13 on 08 Jul 2025** — still within the 20-member EPC ceiling.
- **"Exempt" refers to audit exemption** — a small EPC is exempt from audit — and, critically for this guide's honesty framing, **an EPC's financial statements are not part of the public register in the way larger companies' are**. Revenue, profitability, and balance-sheet health for Combuilder are all **unverifiable from the public record**.
- The EPC structure also **maximises director/ownership privacy**: officer names and shareholding detail sit behind paywalls on every aggregator examined in this pass (§3.5).

Combuilder's public footprint is **thinner than it looks**. The company has a live website, a live LinkedIn page, and active hiring — which makes it *appear* well-documented. But every layer above the registry record is **company-generated or aggregator-written**:

- **The registry record is verified** — UEN, incorporation, status, address, SSIC codes, officer counts, filings. That is the factual backbone of this guide.
- **The website and LinkedIn page are live and extracted** — their words are quoted verbatim and treated as *company claims*, not verified facts.

- **Independent journalism** — **none found** in this pass (recordowl's news monitor: "No recent media coverage found"; targeted searches for Business Times / Straits Times coverage returned nothing).

## 8. Glossary

- **ACRA** — the Accounting and Corporate Regulatory Authority of Singapore; the national regulator that registers companies, issues UENs and maintains the corporate register. Source of the verified registry record for every Singapore company.
- **Aggregator** — a third-party site (companies.sg, opengovsg.com, sgpbusiness.com, recordowl.com, sgbizverify.com, ltddir.com, databasesets) that mirrors ACRA-derived registry data; the verification basis for this guide where several agree.
- **Annual return** — the yearly filing a Singapore company must submit to ACRA confirming its register details; Combuilder's most recent recorded filing was 23 Jul 2025 (aggregator-reported ⚠).
- **BizFile+** — ACRA's official filing and search portal; the authoritative source for officer details, which is paywalled for Combuilder (hence the flagged director field).
- **C4I** — Command, Control, Communications, Computers & Intelligence; the military command-and-control systems domain listed among Combuilder's LinkedIn specialties (company-claimed ⚠).
- **EPC / Exempt Private Company** — a private company limited by shares with ≤20 members and no corporate member; exempt from audit if small; Combuilder's entity type (verified).
- **GeBIZ** — Singapore's government electronic procurement portal; Combuilder is a registered supplier (verified registration ⚠).
- **Incorporation** — the legal act of forming a company; Combuilder's incorporation date is 24 November 1999 (verified).
- **IPOS** — the Intellectual Property Office of Singapore; source of the "combuilder" trademark and the patent applications via aggregators (verified registry observations).
- **LinkedIn** — the professional network; Combuilder's company page (1,963 followers) is the firm's main self-published footprint (page verified live; content company-claimed ⚠).
- **MINDEF** — Singapore's Ministry of Defence; named (with ST Engineering) in Combuilder's LinkedIn About as an early partner (company-claimed ⚠).
- **MyCareersFuture** — Singapore's government jobs portal; Combuilder has active listings as of Aug 2026 (aggregator-reported ⚠).
- **Paid-up capital** — the amount shareholders have actually paid for issued shares; reported as S$1,000,000 (companies.sg) vs S$750,000 (recordowl) — flagged discrepancy ⚠.
- **Peppol** — the Pan-European Public Procurement OnLine e-invoicing network (used in Singapore); Combuilder holds ID SGUEN199907364N (verified registration ⚠).
- **Private limited** — a company whose shareholders' liability is limited to their shareholding; the liability structure underlying "Pte Ltd".
- **Pte Ltd** — the standard abbreviation for "Private Limited" in Singapore company names; **Combuilder Pte Ltd** is the registered name (verified).
- **Registrar / Registry** — the office and record of company registrations; in Singapore, ACRA (and its BizFile+ portal) is the registrar and the registry is the public register the aggregators mirror.
- **SCADA** — Supervisory Control and Data Acquisition; industrial control systems; listed among the author's domain experience in the repo profile (related-era context).
- **SSIC** — the Singapore Standard Industrial Classification; Combuilder's codes are 62021 (IT consultancy, primary) and 62011 (software development, secondary) (verified).
- **ST Engineering** — Singapore's defence-engineering conglomerate; named in Combuilder's LinkedIn About (company-claimed ⚠) and the author's adjacent employer.
- **Struck off** — the registry status of a company dissolved/removed from the register by ACRA (for non-compliance or on application); Combuilder itself is **Live**, but its siblings Combuilder Asia and Combuilder China Capital are **Struck Off** (verified contrast).
- **Team Leader** — the first formal leadership title in the author's public career record, held at Combuilder ~2008–2010 (repo personal guides — personal-history fact).
- **Thin footprint** — the guide's honesty convention: a company whose public record is verified at the registry layer but whose business claims rest on company-reported material; Combuilder's footprint is thin in exactly the dimensions that would independently verify its claims (directors, financials, journalism).
- **UEN** — the Unique Entity Number, Singapore's national business identifier; Combuilder's is **199907364N** (verified) — the entity's safe identifier against the "Combuilder" name cluster.
- **⚠** — the flag marker used throughout this guide for claims that are company-reported, aggregator-written or otherwise unverifiable from the registry or a live primary source.
