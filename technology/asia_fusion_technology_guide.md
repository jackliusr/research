### 1.2 The UEN — 201110062D (Verified)

The Unique Entity Number **201110062D** is verified via all six aggregators consulted. ### 1.3 The Entity Type — Exempt Private Company Limited by Shares (Verified)

The registry classifies Asia Fusion Technology as a **Local Company** with company type description **"Exempt Private Company Limited by Shares"** — verified via companies.sg, opengovsg.com, recordowl.com and sgpbusiness.com, which agree. Key implications, all verified registry/statutory facts:

- **Exempt private company (EPC)** — a private company with no more than 20 shareholders and no corporation holding beneficial interests; the EPC class is exempt from certain disclosure requirements, which is a core reason the firm's financial footprint is thin (§1.7).
- **Shareholder liability is limited to the unpaid amount on shares** — the standard Singapore private-company structure under the Companies Act (Cap. 50).
- **No public financial statements in the ordinary course** — an EPC's accounts are not part of the public register; neither revenue nor profitability is publicly observable.
- **Paid-up capital is SGD 500,000** (companies.sg, recordowl — the two aggregators agree), with 500,000 ordinary shares — a **substantially capitalised** small company, far above the SGD 1 founding capital typical of the young startups in this series (cf. A S$500,000 paid-up capital signals a genuine operating business with real contributed capital — consistent with a long-running services firm, not a shell. companies.sg's own trustworthiness analysis reads the S$500,000 capital and 10-officer count as positive indicators and assigns an overall **"Risk Level: Medium"** on the grounds that the holding-company activity description is broad and operational presence at the registered address is unverifiable (aggregator's analysis — flagged, not this guide's conclusion).

## 8. Glossary

- **ACRA** — the Accounting and Corporate Regulatory Authority of Singapore; the national registrar that incorporates companies and issues UENs. Source of the verified registry record for every Singapore company.
- **Annual return** — the statutory filing a Singapore company must lodge with ACRA each year (shareholders, officers, registered address); a maintained annual-return record is evidence of a company in good standing.
- **Exempt private company (EPC)** — a Singapore private company with ≤20 shareholders and no corporate beneficial ownership; exempt from selected disclosure requirements; Asia Fusion Technology's registry classification (verified).
- **Incorporation** — the legal act of forming a company with the registrar; for Asia Fusion Technology, 27 April 2011 (verified).
- **Live Company** — ACRA status meaning the company is registered and in good standing (filing obligations met); the opposite of "struck off". Asia Fusion Technology's status (verified).
- **Private limited / Pte Ltd** — "Private Company Limited by Shares": shareholder liability limited to the unpaid amount on shares; the standard Singapore corporate form (Companies Act, Cap. 50).
- **Registrar** — the official keeper of the company register; in Singapore, ACRA.
- **Registry** — the public company register (and, by extension, the ACRA-derived aggregator mirrors such as companies.sg, opengovsg.com, recordowl.com that this guide used).
- **SI** — systems integrator; a services firm that builds, integrates and maintains technology solutions for clients across domains, rather than selling one product.
- **SSIC** — the Singapore Standard Industrial Classification; the activity codes a company registers under ACRA (Asia Fusion Technology: 64202 holding companies current; 62019-class software development 2011–2024; 63120 web portals secondary).
- **Struck off** — the registrar's removal of a defunct company from the register (dissolution without formal liquidation); Asia Fusion Technology is **not** struck off (verified).
- **Systems integrator** — see SI; the analytical reading of Asia Fusion Technology's likely business model (§4.1).
- **Thin footprint** — the honest-profile convention of this series: a company whose public record consists of verified registry facts plus self-published claims, with no independent corroboration; every unverifiable claim is flagged ⚠ and nothing is invented to fill gaps.
- **UEN** — the Unique Entity Number, Singapore's national business identifier; Asia Fusion Technology's is **201110062D** (verified) — the entity's safe identifier against brand-name collisions.
- **Ubi belt** — the eastern-Singapore industrial district (Ubi Avenue 3, Ubi Road 1) where the company's registered offices, current (Vertex) and former (Oxley Bizhub 2), sit.
